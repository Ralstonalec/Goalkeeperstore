# Product spec sheets

Fill in one sheet per product **before** you create it in Shopify. Every field on the product page comes from here. Leave a field blank rather than guessing, because blank fields don't render.

Target prices come from your brief. They are **targets, not decisions**. Set final prices once you know landed cost: unit price + shipping + duty + packaging + payment fees.

## Honest-take writing rules

- **Good for / not ideal for:** be specific. Write "Artificial grass and wet grass", not "all conditions". Write "Not ideal for: indoor courts — spray can make palms slick on dusty floors" *only if testing showed it*.
- **Why we made this:** say what you found wrong with the alternatives, then what you did about it. No superlatives.
- **Specs:** facts you can back up with a supplier document or your own measurement.
- **Never write:** "pro-level", "best", "unbeatable", "used by pros", or any claim you can't evidence.
