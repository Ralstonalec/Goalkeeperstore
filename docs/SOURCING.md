# Sourcing from Alibaba without cutting corners

A3GK's promise is "if it isn't good enough to recommend, we don't sell it." Alibaba can deliver that, but only with a process. This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. For chemical products especially, have your labels checked for each market you sell into.

## 1. Shortlist suppliers

- Contact **3–5 suppliers per product**, not one.
- Prefer suppliers with verified status, several years of trading, factory (not trading-company) listings, and third-party audit reports you can read.
- Pay through **Trade Assurance** (Alibaba's escrow-style payment protection). Don't pay by bank transfer outside it.
- Ask each one the same questions in writing and compare the answers:
  - MOQ for custom branding, and MOQ for a first trial order
  - Unit price at MOQ, 2× MOQ and 5× MOQ
  - Sample cost and sample lead time
  - Production lead time and shipping options (air or sea, DDP or not)
  - What exactly they can customise: bottle, label, box, formula, colour, print method

## 2. Samples: test against the benchmark

Order samples from every shortlisted supplier **and** buy the benchmark product (GloveGlu, T1TAN, and so on). Then test them side by side.

**Glove care (spray, wash, deodorizer)**
- Use the same glove model for every test. Put one product on the left glove and the benchmark on the right.
- Grip spray: test tack straight after application, after 20 minutes of use, and on a dry versus a wet palm.
- Wash: run 10+ wash cycles. Check whether the latex dries out, cracks or discolours compared with the benchmark.
- Deodorizer: check odour after 24 hours in a closed bag. Check for residue on the palm.
- Record everything, with photos. Those notes become your "Why we made this" and "Not ideal for".

**Tape:** tear by hand, stickiness after sweat, residue, width and length consistency across the roll.

**Grip socks:** grip pad durability after 10 washes, sizing consistency, and whether the grip pads stay attached.

**Custom padded apparel (builder):**
- Ask for their exact **colour list** and put it in the builder's palette setting.
- Print method. Sublimation allows the full design, so ask about limits on patterns and gradients.
- Available fonts for names and numbers.
- Maximum name length, and logo size and placement limits.
- **Padding:** foam type, thickness, which zones are possible per garment, and whether pads are removable.
- Proof process: will they send a digital proof per order? The builder promises one.
- Real per-piece price at 1, 5, 10 and 25 pieces. That gives you retail prices and team tiers.
- Real production time for a single piece versus a team order. That is your lead time.

**Gloves: parked on purpose.** Your brief says no own-brand gloves until a manufacturer passes a side-by-side test against pro references (Uhlsport, Reusch, Adidas). If you sample gloves from Alibaba in the meantime, test them hard, but don't list them until they pass.

## 3. Chemical products: compliance basics

Grip spray, wash and deodorizer are chemical products, so a few things are non-negotiable:
- Get a **Safety Data Sheet (SDS/MSDS)** from the supplier for every formula.
- **Avoid aerosols.** Aerosols are regulated as dangerous goods in shipping, which makes cross-border delivery expensive or impossible. Use pump or trigger bottles.
- **Labelling rules** depend on the market (for example, CLP in the UK/EU, and consumer chemical labelling rules in the US and elsewhere). Ingredients, hazard statements and a responsible-person address may be required. Check before you print labels.
- Keep a copy of the SDS and the label approval for every batch.

## 4. First order and quality control

- Start with the smallest order that makes the unit price workable.
- Write a **spec sheet** that the supplier signs: formula ref, fill volume, bottle, label artwork file, carton quantity.
- Ask for **pre-shipment photos/video** of the actual batch. For larger orders, pay for a third-party pre-shipment inspection.
- On arrival, spot-check units against your approved sample. If they don't match, don't sell them.

## 5. After launch

- Every post-purchase email and return reason feeds back here. If three keepers say the spray is too sticky in the wet, that goes to the supplier, then into the "You told us" log.
