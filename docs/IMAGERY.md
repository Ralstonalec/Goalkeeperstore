# Imagery: shot list and replacement plan

Until real photos exist, every image slot shows a dark "Photo to come" placeholder that describes the exact shot needed. You can edit each description in the theme editor. Nothing uses stock photos of other brands' products.

## Style

- **Low-key light:** dark backgrounds, one hard light source, rim light on edges. Floodlights and dusk are the look.
- **Real keepers, real pitches:** dirt, rain and wear are good. No studio-clean gloves.
- **Close-ups show the craft:** latex texture, stitching, padding foam, print quality.
- **Motion:** slow shutter, or burst mode mid-dive. A slight motion blur on the ball is fine.
- **Colour:** keep the grade neutral or cool, so the pitch-green accent in the UI does the colour work.

## Getting them cheaply

- A local keeper coach or academy keeper, a floodlit pitch booking, and a friend with a mirror camera and a fast lens (f/1.8) covers shots 1, 8 and 9 in one evening.
- Product shots: a black card background, one LED panel, a spray bottle of water for droplets. A phone in pro mode is enough.
- Video: shoot 120fps slow motion on a modern phone, keep it under 8MB, trim to 8–15s, no audio.

## Replacing placeholders

- **Product photos:** upload to each product in admin. The theme picks them up automatically.
- **Section images:** open the theme editor, select the section, and pick the image. The placeholder disappears as soon as an image is set.
- Always fill **alt text** with what's actually in the photo, for accessibility and SEO.
