We'd rather you spend less and buy right. Here's our honest list. It includes things we don't sell.

## Worth it

- **Two pairs of gloves:** a good match pair and a tougher training pair. Nothing extends the life of your match gloves more.
- **Glove wash, used after every session.** Dirt and sweat kill latex faster than saves do.
- **Padded shorts, if you play on anything harder than soft grass.** Hips first.
- **Finger tape,** if you've jarred fingers before. It's cheap and it works.
- **Grip socks,** if your feet move inside your boots when you push off.

## Usually not worth it

- **Top-range match gloves for training on turf.** Rubber crumb can wear through expensive soft latex in weeks.
- **Fingersaves "just in case".** They're useful after an injury, but for many keepers they cost feel.
- **Buying gloves a size up to grow into.** A loose glove costs you catches.
- **Care "systems" with five bottles.** Wash, grip spray and a deodorizer cover it.
- **Anything sold with a permanent "was" price.** If it's always 40% off, it was never that price.

## Where we fit in

We make the things on the "worth it" list that you buy again and again: glove care, tape and socks. We price them to be bought monthly, not once. Where we can't beat what already exists, we'll say so.
