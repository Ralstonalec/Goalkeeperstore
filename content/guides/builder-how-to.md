## 1. Base

Choose a jersey, shorts, pants, or a full kit (jersey plus shorts or pants). Pick long or short sleeves and the collar. Long sleeves are needed for elbow padding.

## 2. Colours

Choose **main**, **sleeves and side panels**, and **trim** (collar, cuffs, hems). Patterns use your sleeve colour.

Keeper kit should stand out from both teams. Bright colours (volt, orange, pink, sky) are easy for defenders to find and hard for strikers to ignore. If your club has a keeper colour rule, follow it.

## 3. Name and number

- Numbers from 0 to 99. The builder warns you if the number colour will be hard to read from a distance. If it does, add an outline or pick a contrasting colour.
- Names use letters, spaces, full stops, apostrophes and hyphens, up to the length our manufacturer can print.
- The preview approximates the print font. Your proof shows the exact one.

## 4. Crest

Use a simple shield or circle with your initials, or pick **Our club crest**. After you order, we email you for the artwork (vector or high-resolution PNG) and put it on your proof.

## 5. Padding

Pick zones for the jersey (elbows) and the shorts or pants (hips, tailbone, thighs, and knees on pants only). Press **Show padding** to see them on the preview. Not sure what you need? [Read the padding guide →](/blogs/guides/goalkeeper-padding-explained)

## 6. Size and order

Choose sizes. Check the price breakdown and **lead time**, then tick the confirmation box and add to cart. You can **save** your design on your device, or **copy a link** to send to a coach or open on another device.

## What happens next

1. We email a **proof** within [TBC] working days.
2. You reply to approve it or ask for changes. **Nothing is made until you approve.**
3. We make it and ship it. The lead time shown before you paid is the one we work to.
