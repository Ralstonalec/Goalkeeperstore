## Fit data and saved designs

**Glove size calculator.** If you use the calculator on our size guide, your hand measurement, unit and fit preference are saved **in your own browser** (local storage) so the form is pre-filled next time. We don't receive it. Clear your browser data to remove it.

**Kit builder designs.** Designs you save in the builder are stored **in your own browser**. The "Copy link" button creates a link that contains your design; anyone you share that link with can open the design. We don't receive saved designs unless you order.

**When you order custom kit.** Your design (colours, name, number, crest choice, padding, sizes, and a link to reopen it) is attached to your order so we and our manufacturer can make it. Names printed on kit are personal data. We share them with our manufacturer only to produce your order.

**Club crest artwork.** If you send us a crest, we use it only to produce your order and proof. [TBC: how long you keep artwork, e.g. "We keep it for X months so you can reorder, then delete it. Ask us to delete it sooner at any time."]

**Saved fit preferences on your account** *(only once this feature is live)*. If you save sizes, hand measurement or playing preferences to your account, we store them with your customer record to recommend sizes and products. You can view, change or delete them in your account at any time.

**Feedback.** Answers to our post-purchase questions and return reasons are used to improve our products. If we publish a change on our "You told us" page, we never name you without your permission.
