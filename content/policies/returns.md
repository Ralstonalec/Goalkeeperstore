## Custom kit (builder)

Custom kit is made for you, from a proof you approved, so it can't be returned for change of mind or a size you chose.

We'll fix it — remake or refund — if:
- it arrives faulty or damaged, or
- it doesn't match the proof you approved (wrong colour, name, number, crest or padding).

This is why we send a proof and don't make anything until you approve it. Please check sizes against the [size guide](/pages/size-guide) before ordering. If you're unsure, ask us.
